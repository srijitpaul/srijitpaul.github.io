What is a hadron?

A short example showing how equations appear in a note.

Hadrons are particles made of quarks and bound together by the strong interaction. Protons and neutrons are familiar examples; they belong to the family of hadrons called baryons. Mesons, another family, are made of a quark and an antiquark.

Quantum chromodynamics, or QCD, describes the interactions of quarks and gluons. For example, the proton \(p\) has valence quark content

$$ p = uud. $$

This shorthand describes its valence quarks, rather than its full, dynamic internal structure. Lattice QCD provides a way to study hadrons and their interactions through numerical calculations.
